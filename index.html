Bilkul 100%! Aapki files aur client database poori tarah confidential rakha jata hai aur kaam complete hone ke baad kisi ke saath share nahi kiya jata.

Q3. Website, Mobile App aur Logo kitne din me mil jata hai?

Excel cleaning aur Logo concepts same day (3–24 hours) me mil jaate hain. Business websites 3 se 5 din me aur custom Mobile Apps requirement ke hisaab se 7 se 15 din me ready ho jate hain.

Q5. Kya changes / revisions free hain?

Haan. Delivery ke baad agreed scope ke andar changes free hote hain, jab tak aap satisfied na ho jayein.
